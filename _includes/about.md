## Hi, my name is Matt. I am a developer, systems administrator and tech nerd.

I've been tinkering with technology since I was old enough to reach the keyboard.
I always want to explore and understand how things work, and it often leads me on
fun adventures that allow me to expand my knowledge and create something cool in the 
process.

I have lots of experience with different languages but my strongest are
[Java](https://www.java.com/en/), [HTML](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/)/[CSS](https://www.w3.org/Style/CSS/Overview.en.html)
and [Bash](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/). I run a homelab with a focus on 
redundancy and high availability built on [Proxmox](https://www.proxmox.com/en/). 
I host many internal resources as well as [Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us) 
servers with an accompanying [website](https://crewcraftserver.net/). I built my own [3D 
printer](https://print.mattlabs.net) and design and prototype parts for various projects 
from hobby electronics to automotive. I am also a car enthusiast and enjoy working 
on my 1991 Toyota MR2 Turbo.

## Resume

Below, you will find my resume in both code and PDF form. I created my resume in 
[LaTeX](https://www.latex-project.org/), a document typesetting language capable 
of programmatically generating advanced formatting. This makes it easier to maintain 
this than a comparable project in any traditional publishing program. 

Please be patient and keep an eye on the log. As soon as the page loads, the LaTeX
code is automatically compiled into the finished PDF. This happens entirely in
browser, so it can take a short time to process. The LaTeX code can be edited and
recompiled with the red button.
